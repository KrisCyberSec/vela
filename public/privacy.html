Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 20, 2026

Plain English summary: Your health log stays on your device. When you use Ask Vela or Daily brief, Vela sends an anonymized summary to our server so Anthropic's Claude API can answer from your patterns. The summary does not include notes, custom symptom names, exact dates, your name, email, or an advertising identifier.

Vela ("we", "our", or "the app") is a perimenopause and menopause symptom tracking companion operated by Vela Health. This policy covers the Vela iOS app and the velahealth.health website.

1. Data Stored In The App

The app stores information you enter voluntarily:

  • Onboarding answers, including menopause stage, concerns, goals, and guidance preferences
  • Daily logs, including mood, energy, symptoms, severity ratings, sleep, stress, triggers, period context, and free-text notes
  • Experiment progress, Ask Vela conversation history, and feedback on insights

This data is stored locally in the app's device storage. We cannot read it, recover it, or sync it for you.

2. Ask Vela and Daily Brief

Ask Vela and Daily Brief are premium features. When you use them, the app sends a compact summary to Vela's Netlify-hosted API, which forwards it to Anthropic's Claude API. The summary includes aggregated counts and averages from recent logs, plus a weekday-based last-week breakdown. It does not include free-text notes, custom symptom labels, exact dates, real names, email addresses, or advertising IDs.

Our server verifies premium status with RevenueCat before using the model. It also keeps rate-limit, cost-limit, and short-lived cache records in Netlify Blobs. These records use hashed identifiers where practical and are used to operate the feature, prevent abuse, and manage cost.

3. Purchases And Connected Services

  • Apple: processes App Store subscriptions.
  • RevenueCat: manages subscription status using an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipt. No health log contents are sent to RevenueCat.
  • Apple Health: if you grant permission, Vela reads sleep hours. Vela does not write to Apple Health.
  • Sentry: may receive crash and error reports if configured. We use an anonymous app user ID for grouping errors. We do not intentionally send health log contents.

4. Product Analytics

The app may use PostHog for limited product analytics if configured. Autocapture and session recording are disabled. The app sends event names and small counts/status labels around onboarding completion, daily logging, paywall views, purchase status, and premium feature usage or failures. It does not send symptom names, notes, Ask Vela questions, Ask Vela answers, Apple Health values, or log contents. You can turn product analytics off in Settings > Privacy & Data.

5. Website And Waitlist

If you join the waitlist on velahealth.health, we collect your email address, submission time, browser user agent, referring URL, and IP address. Waitlist data is stored on Netlify infrastructure and is used to manage launch communication. We do not sell or rent it.

6. Community Features

The community feature is currently preview-only. If we enable live community features later, posts and replies you publish may be stored on Firebase and visible to other Vela users under an anonymous alias. We will update this policy before enabling live community features.

7. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your real name, phone number, address, contacts, photos, location, or calendar.
  • We do not use ad networks or ad-tracking pixels.
  • We do not collect advertising IDs or device fingerprints.
  • We do not sell data to anyone.

8. Your Rights And Choices

You can delete app data from Settings > Reset all data or by deleting the app. Because app health data stays on your device, deletion is immediate and we do not have a server copy to recover.

For waitlist data, you can request access, correction, deletion, or portability by emailing support@velahealth.health.

9. Security

We use HTTPS for network connections and keep server secrets in protected environment variables. No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you as required by law.

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above reflects the most recent change.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact support@velahealth.health.
