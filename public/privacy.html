Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 14, 2026

Vela ("we", "our", or "the app") is a perimenopause and menopause symptom tracking companion. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Data We Collect

Vela collects the following information that you voluntarily provide:

  • Onboarding questionnaire answers (hormonal stage, symptom preferences, goals)
  • Daily symptom logs (mood, symptoms, severity ratings, free-text notes)

How Data Is Stored

All data is stored locally on your device only, using your browser's or app's local storage. We do not transmit, upload, or store your health data on any external server.

If you delete the app, your data will be permanently lost. We have no ability to recover it.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect personal identifiers (name, email, phone number)
  • We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs
  • We do not collect device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • We do not share any data with third parties

In-App Purchases

Subscription purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipt — no personal health data is shared with RevenueCat.

See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Children's Privacy

Vela is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control. You can delete all data at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Effective date" above.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us at support@velahealth.app.
