Need help? Email support and include your device model, iOS version, and what you were trying to do when the issue happened.

Email: support@velahealth.health

Response target: within 2 business days.

Common Support Topics

  • Subscriptions: Vela subscriptions are processed by Apple. You can manage or cancel in iPhone Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions.
  • Restore purchase: Open Vela, go to the paywall, and tap Restore purchase.
  • Privacy: Your detailed health log is stored on your device. Ask Vela and Daily Brief use anonymized summaries only.
  • Health questions: Vela is informational and is not medical advice. Contact a qualified healthcare provider for medical decisions.
